ORBIT: Space Drone — Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

ORBIT: Space Drone ("ORBIT", "the game") is made by Michael Bartlett. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

No accounts, no personal details

ORBIT has no sign-up and never asks for your name, email, phone number or location. Your scores, unlocks, settings and progress are saved only on your iPhone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ORBIT is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • device information such as device model, operating system and IP address (used to estimate a general location);
  • an advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped, and crash information for ads.

If you don't allow tracking, you still get ads, but they are less personalized. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and EEA), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in the game under Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices.

Google handles this information under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. ORBIT never sees your payment details.

Game Center (Apple)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your high score and Game Center nickname are sent to Apple to show on the "Top Pilots" leaderboard. This is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can turn Game Center off in iPhone Settings.

Sharing

Score card images are created on your iPhone. They are shared only when you choose an app in the share sheet.

Children

ORBIT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the progress saved on your iPhone. For ad data, see Google's policy linked above. You can reset your advertising identifier in iPhone Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
